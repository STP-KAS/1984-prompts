# Cursor

You build 1984. The bot on this desk watches Testnet 10. You do not announce a fund, a sweep, or a price until that check has been done.

The page is https://sixpack.wtf/1984.html. The site repo is STP-KAS/sixpack.wtf. The note is STP-KAS/1984-why-what-how. Read it before you change a sentence about money. STP-KAS/1984, STP-KAS/1984-rails, and this repo point at that note.

## Build

- Keep the welcome gate. New arrival is the test-tab path. If this network has already used today's allowance, the card still closes and the square opens, and no wallet is minted.
- Returning closes the gate and leaves the square open. Who pays stays closed until it is opened. Returning uses Kasware, Kastle, a kaspatest address, or a .kas name when that corner is opened.
- A new arrival writes session storage only. A returning wallet writes the saved store and is not stored as a test tab.
- Draw original art. Stone town, dirt path, fountain, stalls, and the roadster are fine. Do not trace Jagex sprites, and do not use the RuneScape wordmark. The town has no soundtrack. Launch plays one liftoff clip.
- Shops stay Nia's Cafe, Orin's Table, Mara's Groceries, Pike's Roadster, and Lux's Cinema. Venn's bank is a room, not a menu. The roadster waits on the lot. The history in that corner is taller and it scrolls. A hop orbits the Moon, Mars, Jupiter, or Saturn, and jokes go out into space.
- One rail for the whole menu. It opens on POCencept. One Buy button. Prices stay toy cents. tKAS converts at the live quote. POCencept and KUSDT are ledger tags.
- The thin bar lists tKAS, POCencept, and KUSDT. The bank opens on three clerks. Each booth says its name once. Push tKAS, POCencept, or KUSDT. The open clerk swaps into the other two. Locked and purse stay off that screen. The books desk explains them, shows the split, and holds the practice purse and the KUSDT freeze. The wallet signs only a tKAS swap, and only when that wallet's account is the address on the page. A different address does not sign. A mainnet account does not sign. The txid stays in the paste box if the till does not claim it. While the wallet is opening for that swap, the clerk lists the steps and does not rebuild the form. A POCencept swap, a KUSDT swap, or a shop buy asks on the page: you want this for that price, then OK. With Kasware or Kastle logged in, the shop's tKAS rail does not sign.
- On a computer, click the ground to point where you walk, or use the keyboard. Hold the left mouse button to look all the way around. W A S D move the way you look. The arrow keys do too. Esc closes the card, then leaves the room. Stand next to a building and click it to walk in. The card stays shut. The bank card opens when you click a clerk.
