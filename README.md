# Kworld prompts

Cursor builds the page. The bot watches Testnet 10, the ledger, and the words. They are one desk.

The square is [sixpack.wtf/kworld.html](https://sixpack.wtf/kworld.html). What it is: [STP-KAS/kworld](https://github.com/STP-KAS/kworld). Why the three rails exist: [STP-KAS/kworld-rails](https://github.com/STP-KAS/kworld-rails).

## Why these prompts exist

The page, the payments, and the notes drift when each tool invents its own story. These two prompts keep the same limits. Copy [cursor.md](cursor.md) into the builder. Copy [bot.md](bot.md) into the watcher. Neither prompt is permission to post, to take mainnet, or to print a seed.

## What Cursor builds

The welcome gate, the square, the shops, the rules panel, and the bench. Original drawing only. The payment calls stay the ones the server already exposes.

## What the bot watches

The live KAS price, a Testnet-10 balance, a new-arrival fund, and the sweep that follows a closed tab. The bot says when the page and the chain disagree. The bot does not redesign the square while the builder is in it, and the builder does not announce a payment that the bot has not seen.

## Shared limits

- Testnet 10 only. A mainnet wallet is refused.
- Never ask for, print, or commit a seed or a key.
- Do not change the public faucet sentence.
- Do not start a node or retarget a miner for this page.
- Do not vendor Jagex art, a RuneScape wordmark, a private-server engine, or a soundtrack file.
- The rules panel is a stand-in. It is not a SilverScript covenant compiled into the till.
- POCencept and KUSDT are toy tags. tKAS is the coin that moves. KUSDT freeze does not touch the other two.
- KCC-20 is still Draft. There is no spendable layer-1 stable here.
- A vProg guest can sequence a step. This square does not claim the shop spend is that step.
- Do not post on X unless the desk is asked.
